Foreground service: video rendering

Last updated : October 9, 2026 Version : 1.0 Applies to : Cadence (Android)

Cadence uses a foreground service while a video is being rendered on the device. This page documents it for the Google Play foreground service declaration.

02

What the foreground service does

Cadence turns a talking video into an animated vertical video (1080 × 1920). The final video is produced on the device: frames are generated and encoded locally with FFmpeg, which is local media transcoding. A full render takes several minutes.

This is a task the user starts and expects to finish: they tap Render the video, then can leave the app. Without a foreground service, Android would stop the process shortly after the app goes to the background, and the render would be lost.

  • Started by the user: the service starts only when the user taps Render the video (or Quick preview, the first 10 seconds).
  • Visible: a persistent notification titled with the project name shows the progress (percentage and progress bar). Tapping it reopens the app.
  • Short-lived: the service stops as soon as the render ends, fails or is cancelled. A final notification then reports the result.
  • Local only: no upload, no sync with a server. The service does not run in the background for any other reason.
03

Foreground service types and permissions

PermissionUse in Cadence
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSINGLocal transcoding: rendering and encoding the final video on the device
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNCDeclared for the same single service, RenderService, for the same task: local media transcoding of the render (no network synchronisation)
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow the render progress and the end-of-render notification

Both types are declared on one service, RenderService, in the app manifest: android:foregroundServiceType="dataSync|mediaProcessing".

04

How to see it in the app

  1. Open Cadence and sign in.
  2. On the Studio tab, open a demo project (Projet de démonstration).
  3. Tap the export button (top right), then Rendre la vidéo (Render the video).
  4. Press Home and open the notification shade: the render notification with its progress bar is shown.
